Метаданные набора данных
========================================

Описания столбцов
----------------------------------------
grade: grade
major: major
gender: gender
age: age
TME: time management Experience
DOR: Days of recording
TP: Total planned
TC: Total completed
ccc: Completed category count
PCR: Planned completion rate
DIL: Daily independent learning time (min)
DET: Daily extracurricular time (min)
DSE: Daily self-evaluation scores

Расшифровка значений
----------------------------------------

grade:
  21.0 = first iteration
  22.0 = second iteration
  23.0 = third iteration

major:
  1.0 = EDUCATIONAL TECHNOLOGY1
  2.0 = EDUCATIONAL TECHNOLOGY2
  3.0 = digital media

gender:
  1.0 = male
  2.0 = female

TME:
  1.0 = No experience at all.
  2.0 = I've heard of it, but I've never done it myself.
  3.0 = Tried but didn't stick to it.
  4.0 = Have my own personalized experience.
  5.0 = Other
